Common questions

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. It stores everything locally and has no telemetry. The one network call is the one-time embedding model download on first run, unless you choose to configure an external embeddings API.

Does it need a licence key?

No. It does not check your licence or contact any server, on install or afterwards.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, on any devices you personally use. The licence is per person. See the licence.

Is there a team licence?

Not yet. If several people need it, email us and we will work something out.

How do I get updates?

Re-download from your order. All 0.x releases are included. A future major version may be a separate paid upgrade.

What does the encryption cover?

Memories, transcripts, reflections and observations are encrypted at rest by default. The trade-off is that recall uses meaning-based search rather than keyword search.
