Common questions

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. It stores everything locally and has no telemetry. By default it makes no network calls when it runs, because the embedding model is included in the package. Your AI client may send retrieved context to its model provider. Optional external embeddings APIs send content to the provider you choose; a different embedding model may need a download.

Does it need a licence key?

No. It does not check your licence or contact any server, for licence validation. Dependency installation still needs internet access.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, on any devices you personally use. The licence is per person. See the licence.

Can my agent help configure it?

Yes. Give it the included Agent Manual and let it help with MCP registration and your host's capture/recall workflow. Other hosts need adaptation; apply the permissions and approvals your host requires.

How do I open the viewer?

Ask your agent to call open_gui, or use the installed vaultic-mcp-gui command. It needs a local graphical desktop.

Is there a team licence?

Not yet. If several people need it, email us and we will work something out.

How do I get updates?

Re-download from your order. All 0.x releases are included. A future major version may be a separate paid upgrade.

What does the encryption cover?

Memories, transcripts, reflections and observations are encrypted at rest by default. Recall combines semantic search with an encrypted keyword index, including English word-form and prefix matching. Metadata and keyed fingerprints have separate privacy limits. Back up all configured stores and the separate encryption keys.
